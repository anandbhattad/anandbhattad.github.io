# Biography

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois, Urbana Champaign advised by [Prof. David Forsyth](http://luthuli.cs.uiuc.edu/~daf/). I'm interested in understanding scene dynamics -- object-object interaction, 3D reconstruction and inference from videos or multiple static images. I'm also interested in understanding representations learned by deep neural nets. I graduated from the University of Illinois in 2017 with an MS degree in Civil Engineering where I was fortunate to find Prof. David Forsyth and [Prof. Narendra Ahuja](http://vision.ai.illinois.edu/ahuja.html), guiding me into this wonderful discipline of Computer Vision. 

In the summer of 2017, I interned with [Siemens Corporate Technology](https://www.siemens.com/global/en/home/company/innovation/corporate-technology.html) in their Vision Technology and Solutions research team managed by [Jan Ernst](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jan-ernst-2829965). Earlier, I graduated from NITK Surathkal in 2015 with a bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering and did my UG research with [Prof. Katta Venkataramana](http://civil.nitk.ac.in/faculty/kattavenkataramana), [Prof. Nizar Lajnef](https://www.egr.msu.edu/~lajnefni/) and [Prof. K S Babu Narayan](http://civil.nitk.ac.in/faculty/k-s-babu-narayan), whom all have inspired me to pursue a career in research.
