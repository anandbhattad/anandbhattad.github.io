Research Interests

My work aims to bridge the gap between classical perception theories and modern generative models, focusing on advancing computer vision, computer graphics, and computational photography. Notable recent works include recovery of intrinsic images and lighting controls encoded within generative models, revealing their limitations in adhering to projective geometry and developing a latent intrinsic image representation for relighting tasks that challenge traditional intrinsic image decompositions. My long-term goal is to build data-driven methods for computer vision that are rooted in real-world physics, eliminating the need for explicit labeled data.
