Research Interests

I explore the science aspect of modern computer vision. My goal is to bridge classical perception theories with modern generative models, advancing computer vision, computer graphics and computational photography. My research is driven by curiosity to (a) understand how generative models compare to biological perceptual systems and (b) investigate whether these models have learned novel perceptual laws distinct from those known in traditional vision science. Toward this goal, I study where and how perceptual and physical world knowledge is encoded in generative models and how it can be extracted. I believe this will help us interpret and build better generative models. Colloquially I often dub my research as 3P Vision for Pixels, Perception and Physics of Computer Vision.
