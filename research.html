Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of pixels, perception and physics -- what I colloquially call 3P Vision. Thus my new research group at Hopkins is called 3P Vision Group.

My goal is to develop, understand and improve generative models that are perception-driven and physics-aware, thereby advancing the fields of computer vision, computer graphics and computational photography. I work on fundamental questions such as: How do generative models learn to see? What perceptual and physical world knowledge do they encode and where? How can we extract this knowledge to build better models?

I am also deeply curious in understanding how generative models stack up against human perceptual systems. Do they mimic how we see or have they found entirely new ways of perception? Are they creating abstract representations of the visual world or are their representations grounded in physics? Have these models discovered perceptual laws that traditional vision science has yet to discover?

Why does this matter? I believe this will not only help build better generative models with useful controls and improve their knowledge gaps but more importantly, could fundamentally reshape our understanding of visual perception itself.

Other directions my group is exploring:

  • Vision architectures
  • 3D perception
  • Physically grounded generative models
  • Multisensory perception and generation for embodied AI
  • Generative models for computational mechanics
