Research Interests

My research focus is on the intersection of pixels, perception and physics -- what I colloquially call 3P Vision. My goal is to bridge classical perception theories with modern generative models, advancing computer vision, computer graphics and computational photography.

My research is driven by curiosity to understand how generative models stack up against human perceptual systems. Do they mimic how we see or have they found entirely new ways of perception? Are they creating abstract representations of the visual world or are their representations grounded in physics? Have these models discovered perceptual laws that traditional vision science has yet to discover?

To answer these questions, I dig deep into generative models. I look for what perceptual and physical world knowledge they encode and where, and how we can extract them. Why does this matter? I believe this will not only help build better generative models with useful controls but most importantly could fundamentally reshape our understanding of visual perception itself.
