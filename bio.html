About Me

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC), a philanthropically endowed academic computer science graduate institute located on the University of Chicago campus. I am also a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley, hosted by Alexei (Alyosha) Efros.

Earlier, I completed my PhD from Computer Vision Group in the department of Computer Science at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where my advisor was David Forsyth.

Prior to my PhD, I completed master’s degrees in Computer Science and Civil and Environmental Engineering, both at UIUC. Even earlier, I graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from NITK Surathkal, India.

My recent contributions include the recovery of intrinsic images [1, 2] and discovering lighting controls [3] for complex scenes encoded within generative models, showing that generated images do not respect projective geometry [4]. Further, my research demonstrates the emergence of latent intrinsics [5] from training to relight, challenging traditional intrinsic image decompositions methods.
