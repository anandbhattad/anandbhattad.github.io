About Me

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC) located on the University of Chicago campus. I am also a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley, hosted by Alexei (Alyosha) Efros.

Earlier, I completed my PhD from Computer Vision Group in the department of Computer Science at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where my advisor was David Forsyth. Prior to my PhD, I completed master’s degrees in Computer Science and Civil and Environmental Engineering, both at UIUC. Even earlier, I graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from NITK Surathkal, India.
