About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University. I am also a member of the Data Science and AI Institute and the Laboratory for Computational Sensing and Robotics (LCSR).

I earned my PhD from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where I was advised by David Forsyth. During my PhD, I was very fortunate to also be mentored by Derek Hoiem, Svetlana Lazebnik, and Shenlong Wang. After my PhD, I was a Research Assistant Professor at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC), closely working with Greg Shakhnarovich. During that time I spent Summer 2024 as a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley, hosted by Alexei (Alyosha) Efros.

Before my PhD, I completed dual master’s degrees in Computer Science and Civil and Environmental Engineering at UIUC and a bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from NITK Surathkal, India.
